# Kingdom System

The first playable kingdom layer uses Minecraft's persistent scoreboard teams.

- `/kingdom create <name>` creates a named kingdom and registers its founder as its first member.
- `/kingdom info` reports the player's current kingdom and its member count.
- Names must be 3-16 letters, numbers or underscores; kingdom names must be unique.
- Friendly fire is disabled for members of the same team.

This is the foundational faction layer. Invitations, leaving/disbanding, ranks, diplomacy, claimed territory, settlement structures, NPCs, quests and kingdom raids are not implemented yet.
