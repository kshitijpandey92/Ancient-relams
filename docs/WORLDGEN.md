# World generation status

- Mythril and Adamantite generate in Overworld biomes.
- Dragonsteel, Soulstone and Blood Crystal are registered for Nether biome generation, targeting Nether base stone.
- Void Crystal and Celestial Gold are registered for End biome generation, targeting the mod's End Stone replaceables tag.
- Ember, Frost and Storm dragon egg nests have Overworld placement registrations.
- Ruined Keep and Royal Castle structure definitions and template pools exist.
- Royal Castle NBT is generated by `tools/generate_royal_castle.py` during CI.
- Fallback pixel-art textures are generated by `tools/generate_assets.py` during CI.

The Gradle build compiles the biome registrations and the resource validator checks JSON. Actual ore distribution and datapack placement still require a fresh-world in-game test in all three dimensions.
