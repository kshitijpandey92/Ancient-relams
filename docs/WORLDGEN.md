# World generation status

- Mythril and Adamantite ore placed features are registered for Overworld generation.
- Dragonsteel, Soulstone, Void Crystal, Celestial Gold and Blood Crystal have configured/placed feature data, but are deliberately not registered in the Overworld.
- Ember, Frost and Storm dragon egg nests have Overworld placement registrations.
- Ruined Keep and Royal Castle structure definitions and template pools exist.
- The Royal Castle NBT is generated by `tools/generate_royal_castle.py` during CI.
- Fallback pixel-art textures are generated by `tools/generate_assets.py` during CI before resource validation and compilation.

A successful Gradle build and structural JSON/NBT checks do not prove that Minecraft will load the datapack or place structures correctly. Runtime testing in a fresh 26.2 world remains required.
