# Ancient Realms Dragons

Three summonable species are registered: Ember (fire-immune), Frost, and Storm. Each has a custom multipart 3D model: body, neck, head, horns, snout, four legs, tail and two animated wings. Each has its own 128x128 pixel-art texture. They have 180 health, 14 armor, 18 melee damage, 56-block follow range and hostile AI.

Summon:
- `/summon ancientrealms:ember_dragon`
- `/summon ancientrealms:frost_dragon`
- `/summon ancientrealms:storm_dragon`

This first milestone is a ground-based hostile mob. Flight, breath weapons, taming/riding, growth stages, nests/eggs, custom drops and kingdoms remain future work.
